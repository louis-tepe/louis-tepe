## In English

Co-founder & CEO of [Gyroly](https://gyroly.com), a French peer-to-peer marketplace for used tech. Sellers film their device in a guided video, AI assesses its condition, and the payment stays in escrow until the buyer has checked the device. I build the platform hands-on (Next.js, React, TypeScript, PostgreSQL, applied AI). Private beta ([how it works](https://gyroly.com/en/how-it-works)); most of my code lives in private repositories.
